## Individual art direction and sculptural construction
Treat the spreadsheet as a miniature precision-engineered data matrix. Retain the approved DOCX leaf and fold without recoloring its backing. Center a rectangular 3-column × 4-row grid, approximately 68% of face width, with equal column widths and a slightly taller header row. Each cell is a shallow ceramic recess with a softly machined inner edge. The header is muted emerald (#397B68); two isolated body cells use a lighter sage tint, all others warm ivory. Use restrained champagne metal separators thick enough to survive reduction, not a dense wire mesh. Keep every row aligned and every intersection orthogonal in the document plane. No numbers, formulas, charts or extra cells. Place XLSX in the same lower type zone as DOCX, in deep emerald. The visual character is orderly and analytical; the matrix must read before the letters.

Treat these proportions as optical design targets, not visible annotations. Match the approved reference's material roughness, edge highlights and contact shadows. The emblem must be recognizable without relying on its type mark.

Fine microtexture, realistic ceramic roughness, anisotropic brushed metal, controlled highlights and precision-machined edges. No shiny plastic. Material separation must be physically believable.

## Lighting
Seamless warm off-white studio background matching #FCFAF6. Large diffused softbox upper left, delicate secondary fill, subtle ambient occlusion, soft natural contact shadow. Match the approved reference exposure and white balance. No dramatic contrast or hard reflections.

## Quality
Photorealistic premium CGI, physically based rendering, global illumination, exceptional industrial modelling and refined surface finishing. Combine Apple hardware precision, contemporary Italian industrial design and Japanese minimalism. A bespoke professional OS icon, not a toy or illustration. Beautiful at full resolution and legible at 32 × 32 pixels.
